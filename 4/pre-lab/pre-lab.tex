\sct*{電子學實驗(一)\\Analysis of a Common-Source Amplifier 預報}
\ssc*{B14901078 林孜謙、B14901153 沈威宇}
\ssc{實驗目的}
\bit
\item Analyze the DC bias point of a common-source amplifier.
\item Measure the small-signal voltage gain with and without the capacitor \(C_S\).
\item Investigate the frequency response the amplifier.
\eit
\ssc{預備實驗的電路圖}
\cticg[width=0.8\textwidth]{a.jpg}
\ssc{預備量測的數據}
\sssc{DC analysis}
Measure $V_G,V_D,V_S,I_G,I_D,$ and $I_S$. This determines the bias point at which the circuit is operating.
\sssc{Small-signal analysis}
With and without $C_S$, at frequency of 1 kHz to 20 kHz,
\bit
\item measure $v_o$ and $v_{sig}$ with oscilloscope,
\item compute the voltage gain $\abs{\frac{v_o}{v_{sig}}}$, and
\item record the input and output waveforms in Y-t mode.
\eit
