\sct{Exp. 1 Integrator}
\ssc{Circuit}
Measure $v_I$ and $v_O$ waveforms of an op amp where input voltage $v_I$ is connected to $v_-$ through an input resistance $R=1$ k\textOmega (current from $v_I$ to $v_-$), $v_+$ is connected to ground, and $v_O$ is fed back to $v_-$ through a capacitance $C=0.01$ \textmu{}F (103) (current from $v_-$ to $v_O$), under triangle, square, and sinusoidal $v_I$ with amplitude $0.2$.
\sssc{Theoretical results}
$v_O(0^-)$ is the initial voltage across $C$ ($v_O$ relative to $v_-$).

Time domain:
\[v_+=v_-=0\]
\[i_I=\frac{v_I}{R}=i_C=-C\dv{v_O}{t}\]
\[v_O=v_O(0^-)-\frac{1}{RC}\int_0^tv_I(\tau)\dd{\tau}\]
Laplace domain:
\[V_+=V_-=0\]
\[I_I=\frac{V_I}{R}=I_C=-CsV_O+Cv_O(0^-)\]
\[V_O=\frac{1}{s}v_O(0^-)-\frac{1}{RCs}V_I\]
Transfer function: Infinite DC gain. $90$ degrees phase shift.
\[H(s)=\frac{V_o}{V_i}=-\frac{1}{RCs}\]
Unity-gain frequency
\[\frac{1}{2\pi RC}\approx\tx{15.9 kHz}\]
Zero-state responses:
\bit
\item Triangle wave input:
\[v_I(t)=4A\qty(\abs{\frac{t}{T}-\floor{\frac{t}{T}+\frac{1}{2}}}-\frac{1}{4})\qc A,T>0\]
\[v_O(t)=-\frac{AT}{RC}\qty(2s\abs{s}-s)\qc s=\frac{t}{T}-\floor{\frac{t}{T}+\frac{1}{2}}\]
\item Square wave input:
\[v_I(t)=2A\qty(\floor{\frac{t}{T}}-\floor{\frac{t}{T}+\frac{1}{2}}+\frac{1}{2})\qc A,T>0\]
Triangle wave output with amplitude $\frac{AT}{4RC}$
\[v_O(t)=-\frac{AT}{RC}\qty(\abs{\frac{t}{T}-\floor{\frac{t}{T}+\frac{1}{2}}}-\frac{1}{4})\]
\item Sinusoidal input:
\[v_I(t)=A\sin(\frac{t}{T})\qc A,T>0\]
Sinusoidal output 90 degrees leading input with amplitude $\frac{AT}{RC}$.
\[v_O(t)=\frac{AT}{RC}\cos(\frac{t}{T})\]
\eit

\sct{Exp. 2 Differentiator}
\ssc{Circuit}
Measure $v_I$ and $v_O$ waveforms of an op amp where input voltage $v_I$ is connected to $v_-$ through an input capacitor $C=0.01$ \textmu{}F (103) (current from $v_I$ to $v_-$), $v_+$ is connected to ground, and $v_O$ is fed back to $v_-$ through a resistor $R=1$ k\textOmega (current from $v_-$ to $v_O$), under triangle, square, and sinusoidal $v_I$ with amplitude $0.2$.
\ssc{Theoretical results}
$v_I(0^-)$ is the initial voltage across $C$ ($v_I$ relative to $v_-$).

Time domain:
\[i_I=C\dv{v_I}{t}=i_f=\frac{-v_O}{R}\]
\[v_O=-RC\dv{v_I}{t}\]
Laplace domain:
\[I_I=CsV_I-Cv_I(0^-)=I_f=\frac{-V_O}{R}\]
\[V_O=-RCsV_I+RCv_I(0^-)\]
Transfer function: Infinite gain at $\omega=\infty$. $-90$ degrees phase shift.
\[H(s)=\frac{V_o}{V_i}=-RCs\]
Unity-gain frequency
\[\frac{1}{2\pi RC}\approx\tx{15.9 kHz}\]
Responses:
\bit
\item Triangle wave input:
\[v_I(t)=4A\qty(\abs{\frac{t}{T}-\floor{\frac{t}{T}+\frac{1}{2}}}-\frac{1}{4})\qc A,T>0\]
Square wave with amplitude $\frac{4ARC}{T}$
\[v_O(t)=-\frac{8ARC}{T}\qty(\floor{\frac{t}{T}}-\floor{\frac{t}{T}+\frac{1}{2}}+\frac{1}{2})\qc\frac{2t}{T}\notin\bbZ\]
\item Square wave input:
\[v_I(t)=2A\qty(\floor{\frac{t}{T}}-\floor{\frac{t}{T}+\frac{1}{2}}+\frac{1}{2})\qc A,T>0\]
Dirac delta sum output
\[v_O(t)=2ARC\sum_{n\in\bbZ}\delta(t-nT)-\delta(t-\qty(n+\frac{1}{2})T)\]
\item Sinusoidal input:
\[v_I(t)=A\sin(\frac{t}{T})\qc A,T>0\]
Sinusoidal output 90 degrees lagging input with amplitude $\frac{ARC}{T}$.
\[v_O(t)=-\frac{ARC}{T}\cos(\frac{t}{T})\]
\eit

\sct{Exp. 3 Frequency Responses (II) Integrator with feedback resistor}
\ssc{Circuit}
Measure frequency response of the circuit in Exp. 1 Integrator with the capacitor $C$ replaced with a capacitor $C=0.001$ \textmu{}F (102) and a resistor $R_F$ in parallel connection under
\[v_I=0.2\sin(2\pi ft),\quad f=\tx{100 Hz}\sim\tx{1 MHz}\]
and sketch the Bode magnitude plot.
\ssc{Theoretical results}
Laplace domain:
\[V_+=V_-=0\]
\[I_I=\frac{V_I}{R}=I_F=-\qty(\frac{1}{R_F}+Cs)V_O+Cv_O(0^-)=-\frac{1+R_FCs}{R_F}V_O+Cv_O(0^-)\]
\[V_O=\frac{R_FC}{1+R_FCs}v_O(0^-)-\frac{\frac{R_F}{R}}{1+R_FCs}V_I\]
Transfer function: STC low-pass with cutoff frequency $\frac{1}{2\pi R_FC}$ and DC gain $\frac{R_F}{R}$.
\[H(s)=\frac{V_o}{V_i}=-\frac{\frac{R_F}{R}}{1+R_FCs}\]
